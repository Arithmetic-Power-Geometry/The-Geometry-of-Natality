# GoN G1 targeted prior-art evidence
Do not claim novelty for state-graph planning or action discovery without addressing:
1. Graph Transformation Planning via Abstraction (Steffen Ziegert, 2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.7933 — graph transformation planning is established.
2. A novel approach to solve AI planning problems in graph transformations, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence 92 (2020), 103684, DOI 10.1016/j.engappai.2020.103684 — explicitly plans in graph transformation systems.
3. Computing Contingent Plan Graphs using Online Planning, ACM Transactions on Autonomous and Adaptive Systems 16(1), article 3488903 (2022), DOI 10.1145/3488903 — partial-observation and online contingent planning.
4. GROOVE graph transformation/model checking tool: https://groove.cs.utwente.nl/ — established state-space exploration, isomorphism detection, model checking.
5. Graphplan, https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~avrim/graphplan.html — classical planning baseline.
Sources checked via public publisher/project pages 2026-10-18. These are overlap flags, not a systematic literature review.
G0 outcome: implemented finite prerequisite-forest grammar is reducible to precedence-constrained knapsack. G1 outcome: no independent GoN novelty established. G3: only small deterministic oracle-vs-exhaustive-vs-greedy correctness checks, not publication-scale benchmarks.
