# Research gates and adversarial review
1. Do not claim 'new geometry' merely by naming existing reachability quantities.
2. Compare structural signatures: |V|, |E|, degree sequences, SCC-size multiset, weighted SCC condensation, transitive closure, and spectral data.
3. Distinguish failure of a coarse summary from failure of a complete invariant.
4. Test S2 (minimum full saturation budget) and S3 (weighted condensation one-edge optimum) independently.
5. Add generative-state-space tests: accessibility-only, creation-only, realized creation, identity embeddings, and action-composition consistency.
6. For k>=2 use exhaustive ground truth on small instances and exact or bounded solvers on larger instances.
7. Record counterexamples to every overbroad claim, including restricted actions and nonmonotone interventions.
8. Require novelty search and independent proof review before calling any result original.
9. Preserve references.bib; append checked bibliographic records with source and verification date.
10. A manuscript should clearly separate definitions, proven facts, hypotheses, computational observations and unresolved questions.
